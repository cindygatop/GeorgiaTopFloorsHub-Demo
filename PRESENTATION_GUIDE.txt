GEORGIA TOP FLOORS HUB — QUICK PRESENTATION GUIDE

ARCHITECTURE
One platform / one database, with different views and permissions.

OFFICE / ADMIN
- Dashboard
- Jobs
- Calendar
- Customers
- Crews
- Internal operational information

CREW / EMPLOYEE
- Today's assigned jobs
- Address, arrival window, service, sqft
- OTW, Arrived, In Progress, Completed
- Stain/color
- Materials
- Notes / Report Problem
- Future GPS starts only at OTW and ends at Arrived

CUSTOMER
- Only their own property/project
- Next visit
- OTW / arrival tracking
- Project progress
- Stain/color
- Invoice/payment when allowed
- Care & warranty
- "I won't be home" notice

CONTRACTOR PRIVACY
For Alpha or another contractor:
- homeowner can see project progress, schedule, OTW and project details
- homeowner does NOT see internal prices, subcontractor prices, margins, purchase orders or internal notes

PRODUCTION
These may look like separate apps to each user, but they share the same secure backend and database.
Authentication/roles decide what each person can access.
