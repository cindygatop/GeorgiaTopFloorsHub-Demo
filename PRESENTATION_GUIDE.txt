GEORGIA TOP FLOORS HUB — PRESENTATION GUIDE v3

SIMPLE IDEA TO EXPLAIN

"Today some information is spread between contacts, messages, files, calendars and different systems. The Hub would turn that into one organized company database. The goal is not just to store information, but to make it useful."

DATABASE

Contacts can be classified as:
- Past Customer
- Current Customer
- Lead
- Estimate Not Closed
- Builder
- Contractor
- Partner

Each profile can keep:
- phone / email / address
- lead source
- last service
- last contact
- project history
- internal notes
- communication permission / DO NOT CONTACT

This allows targeted follow-up instead of sending generic messages to everyone.

SUBCONTRACTORS

Each subcontractor gets one profile with:
- company
- trade / skills
- service area
- availability
- active / inactive
- jobs completed
- notes
- W-9
- Certificate of Insurance
- Workers' Comp when applicable
- licenses / certificates
- subcontract agreement

Documents with expiration dates can automatically become:
- Expiring Soon
- Expired

Core benefit:
"Today the information depends on us remembering it. The idea is for the information to warn us."

PROJECT DIGITAL FOLDER

Each job can later have:
- Before Photos
- Stain Samples
- Customer Selection
- Service Order
- Estimate
- During Work
- Issues Found
- After Photos
- Signed Documents
- Invoice
- Care & Maintenance

ONE PLATFORM, DIFFERENT ACCESS

Office sees the full operation.
Crew sees only assigned work and field actions.
Customer sees only their own project.
Contractor-referred homeowners can have pricing/internal information hidden.

IMPORTANT
This is a static presentation prototype with sample data. Production will require authentication, permissions, secure database/storage and real integrations.
