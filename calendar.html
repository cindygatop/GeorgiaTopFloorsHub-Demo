To activate two-way sync

1. Set up Google Cloud OAuth consent and Calendar API.
2. Connect the correct business calendar from the secure backend.
3. Store event IDs, OAuth refresh credentials and sync tokens securely.
4. Process webhook notifications, retries, conflicts and expiration/renewal.
5. Confirm updates before writing to Google, then log the result.
